# User Interviews

> 5 interviews with people who work in chat all day. Ask about their _current_ behaviour, never "would you use a tool that...". See _The Mom Test_.

## Recruiting

Send individually, not as a broadcast — a personal DM reads as a real ask; a group message reads as a survey. Aim for 5 people who actually live in chat day to day; yoomind colleagues count, so does anyone outside work with a similarly chat-heavy job (one or two non-yoomind data points helps you tell "yoomind's specific culture" apart from "the actual problem").

**Message template** (adapt the opener to how you'd actually talk to that person):

> Hey [name] — I'm doing a bit of research on my own time on how work actually gets tracked (or doesn't) when it's discussed in chat rather than written down somewhere. Not selling anything, don't need to prep — could I grab 15–20 min this week to ask about how it actually goes for you? Happy to work around your calendar.

If asked what it's for: "a side project I'm looking into — I'll tell you more once I've actually talked to people, don't want to bias what you tell me." That's honest, not evasive — the Mom Test explicitly warns that describing the idea before the interview changes the answers.

**Logistics:**

- Book 20 min, expect to use 15 — respecting their time is what gets you a real answer instead of a rushed one.
- Take notes during, not after — memory reconstructs answers to fit what you expected to hear.
- Do the first one as a trial run and re-read the Script below immediately after; it's normal to catch yourself leading the witness on interview #1.

## Script

Ask in this order. Let them talk — the goal is their story, not a checklist confirmed.

- **Q0 — the competitive check (new, added after the competitive scan).** "Do you use Gemini in Google Chat, or any bot like Hana that turns chat into tasks?" If yes: **"Walk me through the last time you actually used it. What did it miss, or why didn't you reach for it?"** This is the question that tests whether the wedge in [competitive-analysis.md](competitive-analysis.md) — personal, passive, no shared bot, no admin approval — is something they actually care about, or a distinction only a builder would draw. If they'd happily use a Hana-style shared bot, say so plainly in the finding below; that's a result, not a failure.
- **Q1.** "Walk me through the last time something agreed in chat got forgotten. What happened?"
- **Q2.** "How do commitments made in chat currently end up in your tracker, if they do?"
- **Q3.** "How much time would you guess you spend re-explaining or re-asking things that were already said in chat?"
- **Q4.** (If they code-switch languages) "Does anything change about how you'd expect this to work across German/English in the same thread?"
- **Q5.** "If you could wave a wand, what's the actual annoying part — not the idea, the current annoyance?"

Never pitch Cairn before Q5. If they ask what you're building, deflect until the interview is done.
